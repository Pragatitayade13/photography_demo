# VS-09 — Photographer About / Profile Showcase

**Project:** Photography Showcase Website
**Vertical Slice:** VS-09
**Module:** Public Photographer About / Profile
**Depends On:** VS-04 Category Management, VS-05 Photo Management, VS-06 Project/Gallery Management, VS-07 Homepage CMS, VS-08 Public Portfolio

---

## 1. Slice Goal

VS-09 creates a premium **About the Photographer** experience.

The visitor should understand:

* Who the photographer is
* Their photography philosophy
* Their story
* Their experience
* Their specialties
* Their achievements
* Their working style
* Where they work
* What services they offer
* Why someone should hire them
* How to contact them

The page should feel like a **personal brand website**, not a resume page.

### Visitor journey

```text
Portfolio
   ↓
About
   ↓
Photographer Story
   ↓
Style & Expertise
   ↓
Experience / Achievements
   ↓
Services
   ↓
Why Work With Me
   ↓
Contact Photographer
```

# 37. Business Rules

### BR-ABOUT-001

Only authenticated admin users can modify About content.

### BR-ABOUT-002

Draft changes must not affect the public page.

### BR-ABOUT-003

Only one published About version should be active.

### BR-ABOUT-004

Hidden sections must not render publicly.

### BR-ABOUT-005

Hidden services must not render.

### BR-ABOUT-006

Hidden testimonials must not render.

### BR-ABOUT-007

Deleted referenced images must not produce broken UI.

### BR-ABOUT-008

Only published projects can appear in Personal Work.

### BR-ABOUT-009

Statistics are optional.

### BR-ABOUT-010

Empty sections should not render.

### BR-ABOUT-011

Section ordering must persist.

### BR-ABOUT-012

Public API must never expose admin-only metadata.

# 55. UAT — Profile

**Given:** Photographer has configured their profile.

**When:** Visitor opens `/about`.

**Then:** Photographer's name, title, profile image and introduction should appear correctly.

---

# 56. UAT — Story

**Given:** Photographer has written their story.

**When:** Visitor reaches the Story section.

**Then:** The story should display with the configured image and layout.

---

# 57. UAT — Services

**Given:** Photographer has configured three services.

**When:** Visitor opens the About page.

**Then:** All three visible services should be displayed.

---

# 58. UAT — Testimonials

**Given:** Photographer has five testimonials and hides one.

**When:** Visitor views Testimonials.

**Then:** Only four testimonials should be shown.

---

# 59. UAT — Draft

**Given:** A published About page exists.

**When:** Photographer modifies their biography and saves a draft.

**Then:** Visitors should continue seeing the existing published biography.

---

# 60. UAT — Publish

**Given:** A draft About page exists.

**When:** Photographer publishes it.

**Then:** Visitors should see the updated About page.

---

# 61. UAT — Responsive

**Given:** Visitor opens `/about` on a mobile device.

**Then:**

* No horizontal scrolling
* Images fit the viewport
* Typography remains readable
* CTA remains accessible
* Animations remain smooth
* Navigation works
